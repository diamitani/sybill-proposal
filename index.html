4. Sales Playbook for Sybill

Operating rules that make the workflows useful instead of impressive-but-ignored.

Primary funnel

  • Account enters system (inbound/sourced)
  • Intent and fit classified in CRM
  • Qualified lead routed with context
  • Demo/discovery booked with clean follow-up
  • Deal enters evaluation with note discipline
  • Outcomes feed back into messaging + prompts

Ownership rules

  • Commercial inbound never sits unclassified
  • RevOps owns routing logic, not exceptions
  • Sales owns response quality + conversion
  • Prompt changes documented and versioned
  • Lost-deal insights feed outbound monthly

Core personas

RevOps ownerWants CRM hygiene, routing trust, repeatable systems.
Sales leader / CROCleaner pipeline, better coaching signals, less management drag.
Sales managerFaster deal inspection, coaching from patterns not anecdotes.
AE championFaster prep, cleaner CRM, more selling time.
