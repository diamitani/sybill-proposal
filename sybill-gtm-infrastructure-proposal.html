Outbound architecture

Prospect outbound automation workflow

Step 1

Lead feed

Upload target accounts or trigger from intent, lists, or CRM views.

Step 2

Clay enrichment

Find contacts, titles, emails, firmographics, signals, and account metadata.

Step 3

Research agent

Build account brief, likely pain points, recent trigger events, and relevance angle.

Step 4

Messaging agent

Draft email variant based on ICP, persona, proof point, and CTA rule.

Step 5

Send / sequence

Push to outreach platform or send through workflow with guardrails.

Step 6

Track + report

Sync status, replies, meetings, bounce rate, and workflow health back to dashboard.

Core Phase 1 build: one production-grade email automation that starts with leads and ends with a personalized draft ready for sequence enrollment.

Inbound architecture

Prospect inbound automation workflow

Modeled on proven inbound triage systems

The Atlas case study in this project shows a working pattern: form intake, AI intent classification, CRM correction logic, and same-second personalized draft response.

  • Capture every inbound form with webhook reliability.
  • Classify real intent from free-text message, not just dropdown selection.
  • Write clean CRM state only when correction is needed.
  • Draft the first response in brand-safe tone with routing context.

Sybill-specific application

  • Route by buyer type: AE leader, RevOps, sales manager, founder-led sales, CS.
  • Separate demo intent from partnerships, support, candidate, and general inbound.
  • Auto-assign owner, SLA, lifecycle stage, and follow-up status.
  • Push response draft into CRM and notify the right rep instantly.

Post-call architecture

Post-sales-call automation workflow

Capture

Meeting event or recording triggers a workflow to ingest transcript, participants, deal context, and CRM record.

Structure

AI extracts summary, action items, blockers, MEDDPICC/BANT notes, next meeting, risks, and buying signals.

Execute

Send follow-up draft, update CRM, open tasks, notify manager, and log enablement insights for reuse.
