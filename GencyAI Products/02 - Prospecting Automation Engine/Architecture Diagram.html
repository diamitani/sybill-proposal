Prospecting Automation Engine

Intent-to-Outreach™

An autonomous prospecting engine that pulls intent signals daily, runs them through 8 ICP filters, routes to reps by territory, and ships to enrichment — all before reps log in.

Data Flow

1
7am trigger fires Schedule kicks off — no manual intervention. Workflow self-bootstraps.
2
Intent capture Pull every intent-flagged company from yesterday's signals (200+ records typical).
3
Filter cascade 8 filters in sequence. Each drop is logged. Console output shows what survived and why.
4
Territory routing Assign to one of 6 rep pods based on location. Round-robin within multi-rep pods.
5
Enrichment + audit Post to Clay (parallel enrichment) and upsert to datatable (audit log) — same payload.

Components

01

Schedule Trigger

Daily run at 7am. No human kickoff. Pulls yesterday's intent signals automatically.

02

Intent Capture

Queries HubSpot for any company hitting ZoomInfo or Factors ABM intent yesterday.

03

Filter Cascade

8 sequential filters — each does one job. Easy to debug, easy to extend.

04

APAC Detection

3-tier detection: location fields → domain TLDs → company-name keywords.

05

Round-Robin Engine

Territory-aware assignment with externalized pod config. 6 pods supported.

06

Clay Webhook

Posts qualified leads to Clay for people enrichment + outreach automation.

07

Datatable Audit

Every assignment logged for ops visibility, reporting, and dedup.
