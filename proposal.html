01 — Outbound Prospecting Orchestrator

A scheduled pipeline replacing spreadsheet-based account qualification with automated enrichment and sequencing.

Step 1

Lead Feed

Upload targets or trigger from intent signals and CRM views.

Step 2

Clay Enrichment

Find contacts, titles, emails, firmographics, and signals.

Step 3

Research Agent

Build account brief, pain points, trigger events.

Step 4

Messaging Agent

Draft email based on ICP, persona, and CTA rules.

Step 5

Send / Sequence

Push to outreach tool with guardrails and round-robin.

Step 6

Track + Report

Sync status, replies, meetings, bounce rate back to dashboard.

Core deliverable: One production-grade email automation that starts with leads and ends with a personalized draft ready for sequence enrollment. ICP filters exclude competitors (Gong, Clari, Fathom) and low-fit accounts automatically.

02 — Inbound Triage Engine

Autonomous webhook-to-CRM pipeline that reads form data, classifies intent via LLM, and alters CRM state in under 30 seconds.

How it works

  • Webhook captures every HubSpot form submission instantly.
  • LLM classifies real intent from free-text (Sales, Support, Partnership, Career).
  • Writes clean CRM state: ai_reason_for_contact, lifecycle stage correction.
  • Auto-generates consultative, brand-safe draft response on the contact record.

Sybill-specific routing

  • Route by buyer type: AE leader, RevOps, sales manager, founder-led sales.
  • Separate demo intent from partnerships, support, candidates, general inbound.
  • Auto-assign owner, SLA, lifecycle stage, and follow-up status.
  • Push response draft into CRM and notify the right rep instantly.

03 — Post-Call Intelligence (Meta-Sybill)

Drinking our own champagne. Sybill's meeting outputs trigger parallel AI agents that execute downstream actions automatically.

Capture

Execute

Why this matters: Sybill's own product story is turning context into action. The internal sales process should mirror that promise — every call ends with the same standard of execution, not a rep-by-rep memory test.
